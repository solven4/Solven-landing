Why it matters

Most traders fail a funded-account challenge for behavioral reasons, not strategy ones. EDGE turns raw trade history into 8 objective scores and a real pass-probability estimate, so you find the leak before it costs you a challenge fee.

How to use it

1
Connect your MT5 account — positions and history sync automatically.
2
Once you have 5+ closed trades, open your Trading DNA report.
3
Run the Prop Firm Risk Room before you pay for a real challenge attempt.
4
Ask the AI Coach to explain any score you don't understand.
